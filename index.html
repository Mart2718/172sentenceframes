    Classroom use and sources

    Select general starters or a textbook objective, then choose a discourse category. Select up to three frames and present them on the board. If you do not select frames, presentation displays the first one or three. Students complete the blanks with their own mathematical reasoning.

    The topic list follows the 39 Math 172 textbook objectives supplied by the instructor. Frames are original scaffolds aligned with those objectives. General categories incorporate the instructor’s sentence-frame collection and Academic Language Frames/Sentence Starters for Mathematical Discourse.
